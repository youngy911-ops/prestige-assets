Questions They Might Ask
"How long did this take to build?"
About two weeks in my own time outside work hours. It's live now at assetbookintool.com.
"What if the AI gets something wrong?"
There's a review step — nothing goes into Salesforce until a person confirms it. More reliable than manual entry where a typo never gets flagged.
"What does IT need to do?"
Set up a sandbox environment — a test copy of Salesforce — and send me a pair of credentials. Probably 30 minutes of their time. Everything else is on me.
"Where does the data go?"
Everything stays in Australia — photos and data are hosted in Sydney. Not locked to one AI provider either.
"How much does it cost to run?"
Under $900 a year all in — AI fees, hosting, domain. At 100 assets a week that's about $2-5 a week in AI costs.
"What asset types does it handle?"
Nine categories — trucks, trailers, earthmoving, agriculture, forklifts, marine, caravans, vehicles with damage detection, and general goods.

The API connection we're approving is the foundation for broader Salesforce automation — auction creation, consignor updates, settlement automation. The book-in app is the proof of concept.
