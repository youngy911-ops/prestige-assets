The Ask

Sandbox access so I can build the Salesforce integration.

Today: copy-paste ready output. Goal: direct write into Salesforce, no copy-paste step.

What I need:

  1. Access to a Salesforce sandbox for development and testing
  2. A small amount of IT support to set up the app connection in the sandbox. I can provide exactly what's needed.
  3. A small pilot group to test with once it's built
  4. A review meeting at the end to decide on full rollout

The Salesforce setup is minimal. All the actual build is on me.

In the meantime, a CSV import version can be live within days. Staff aren't waiting while the full integration is built.

Risk & Data

The questions you'll want answered.

Data & privacy

  • All data stays in Australia: photos, extracted data, Salesforce records
  • Not locked to one AI provider. Happy to use whichever model IT is comfortable with
  • If there are detailed IT questions on infrastructure, I'll take them on notice and follow up
  • Every Salesforce write is done by the logged-in user. Nothing happens automatically or anonymously

Safeguards

  • Bad photos: flags missing fields, doesn't invent data
  • Wrong extraction: review step catches it before anything is saved
  • AI outage: clear error shown, fall back to manual entry as normal
  • Staff confirm everything before it goes into Salesforce
