Plain English — Know This Cold
What is an API?
A way for two software systems to talk to each other directly. Right now the app produces copy-paste output — a human has to move that data into Salesforce. With API access, the app sends it directly, no human step. Like the difference between emailing someone a document vs giving them live access to the file.
What is a sandbox?
A copy of Salesforce used for testing. Nothing in it is real or live. IT creates one, I build and test against it, and nothing touches the real Salesforce until we've verified it all works. Standard practice — IT will know exactly what it means.
What is OAuth? (if IT asks)
It's the login method — the same thing you use when you click "Sign in with Google" on a website. Each user logs into Salesforce through the app using their own credentials. Means every action is tracked to the person who did it. I wouldn't raise this unless IT specifically asks.
What does "External Client App" mean? (if IT asks)
It's Salesforce's term for a third-party app that connects to their system. IT creates it inside Salesforce, it generates a pair of keys, they send me the keys, I plug them in and the connection works. It's their standard process for integrations — they'll know the steps.
What is Supabase / Vercel? (if IT asks)
Supabase is the database (hosted in Sydney). Vercel is where the app is hosted. Both are standard cloud platforms used by thousands of companies. The relevant thing for IT is that all data storage is in Australia.
What does "not used for training" mean?
When you use OpenAI's API (the paid business version), your data is not used to train their AI model. It goes in, comes back out, gets deleted after 30 days. It's different to using the free ChatGPT website where your chats can be used to improve the model.
