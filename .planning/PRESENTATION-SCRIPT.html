If They Ask
"What if it gets something wrong?"
There's a review step, nothing goes into Salesforce until a person confirms it. It's actually more reliable than manual entry, where a typo never gets flagged.
"Why not buy an off-the-shelf tool?"
Nothing on the market is built around Slattery's Salesforce schema and description format. Anything off-shelf would still need custom work, plus ongoing licensing. This is built exactly for how we work.
"What if you leave?"
That's what the pilot is for. If it proves value, it gets formalised, documented and handed over. Right now it's a proof of concept; the pilot is how we decide whether to make it official.
"Where does our data go?"
Everything stays in Australia, photos, data, Salesforce records. Not locked to one AI provider either, happy to use whatever IT is comfortable with.
"How much does it cost?"
Under $900 a year all in. At 100 assets a week, that's about $2–$5 a week in AI costs plus hosting. Trivial even at much higher volume.
"What does IT actually have to do?"
Set up a sandbox, create the app connection inside it, and send me a couple of credentials. Probably 30 minutes total. Everything else is on me.
Technical IT question you don't know
"Good question, I'll find out and come back to you on that."
